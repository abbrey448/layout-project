Sports

Football

Football is played by two teams with eleven players. The aim is to score more goals than the other team. Football is one of the most popular sports in the world. It helps players stay fit, improve teamwork, and enjoy friendly competition.

Basketball

Basketball is played by two teams of five players. The players dribble, pass, and shoot the ball into a hoop to score points. It is a fast and exciting game that improves fitness and teamwork.

Swimming

Swimming is a sport that takes place in water. It helps build strong muscles and keeps the body healthy. Many people enjoy swimming because it is fun, relaxing, and good exercise.

Running

Running is a simple sport that almost everyone can enjoy. It improves heart health, increases energy, and keeps the body active. Running races are common in schools and sports competitions.
